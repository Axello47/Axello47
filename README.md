## / ABOUT

I’m **Axel**, also known around the internet as **Axello**.

I’m a student developer who spends a suspicious amount of time turning ideas into websites, interfaces, experiments and little digital worlds.

Most of my work sits somewhere between **development and visual design**. I like interfaces that feel intentional, responsive and slightly different from everything else.

```text
currently
└── building web experiences
    ├── frontend
    ├── product interfaces
    ├── visual systems
    ├── experiments
    └── learning by shipping
```
